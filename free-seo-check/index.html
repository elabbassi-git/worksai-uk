About the free check

Is this an automatic score?

No. A person on our team looks at your website and your Google presence and writes the report. That is why it takes up to 2 working days.

What will the report cover?

Speed on mobile, page titles and descriptions, how clearly your services are described, your Google Business Profile, reviews, and the three changes we would make first.

I don’t have a website. Can I still use this?

Yes. Tick “I don’t have a website yet” and we will send you a free homepage mock-up idea instead, showing what a simple site for your business could look like.

Will you add me to a mailing list?

Only if you tick the optional box. Otherwise we use your details just to send the report and answer any questions.
