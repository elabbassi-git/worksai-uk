## Signs your website needs attention

You might need a redesign if several of these are true:

- It looks broken or awkward on a phone
- It takes more than a few seconds to load
- You can't update it yourself, or you're scared to
- It hasn't brought in an enquiry you can trace in months
- Your services, prices or photos are out of date
- It doesn't appear when you search for what you do in your area
- Your browser shows a "not secure" warning

## Fix or rebuild?

**Fix it** when the design is reasonably modern and the problems are specific: slow images, missing page titles, a broken form, thin service pages. A focused round of fixes can make a big difference for less money.

**Rebuild it** when the problems are structural: the site isn't mobile-friendly, the platform is out of date or insecure, every change needs a developer, or the content needs rewriting anyway. Patching an old site can cost more over time than starting again.

## Speed matters more than it used to

Visitors on phones leave quickly if a page is slow, and Google uses page experience signals, including Core Web Vitals, when ranking. The usual causes are oversized images, heavy themes and page builders, too many plugins and cheap hosting. A modern build avoids most of these from the start.

## Protecting your Google rankings during a redesign

The biggest risk in any redesign is losing traffic you already have. To avoid it:

1. List every page on the old site and which ones get traffic.
2. Keep or improve the content on pages that rank.
3. Redirect every old address to the most relevant new page (a 301 redirect).
4. Keep page titles and headings at least as clear as before.
5. Submit the new sitemap in Google Search Console and monitor it for a few weeks.
