## Step 1: Create your Google Business Profile

Go to Google Business Profile and search for your business name. If it already exists, claim it. If not, create it. Choose the most accurate **primary category** (for example, "Electrician" rather than "Contractor"), and add your phone number, website and hours.

If customers come to you, add your address. If you go to them, set up as a **service-area business**: hide the address and list the towns or postcodes you cover.

## Step 2: Verify it

Google needs to confirm your business is real. It may ask for a short video of your premises or vehicle and tools, a phone or text code, or a postcard. Your profile won't show fully until this is done.

## Step 3: Fill in everything

Profiles with complete information tend to perform better. Add services with descriptions, a business description, opening hours (including bank holidays), photos of your work, your logo and attributes such as accessibility.

## Step 4: Get a simple website

Your profile should link to a website that confirms the same details. It doesn't need to be big: a home page, a page per service and a contact page are enough to start. See our guide for [businesses with no website yet](/small-business-website/).

## Step 5: Tell Google your website exists

Set up Google Search Console, verify your domain and submit your sitemap. This helps Google find and index your pages, and shows you which searches you appear for.

## Step 6: Start collecting reviews

Ask happy customers for a review, using the review link in your profile. Send it by text or WhatsApp straight after the job, while they're pleased. Reply to every review.

## Step 7: Keep it active

Post an update or photo every week or two, answer questions, and update your hours for holidays. An active profile signals to customers, and to Google, that you're open for business.
