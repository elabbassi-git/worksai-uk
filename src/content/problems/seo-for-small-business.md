## What SEO actually is

Search engine optimisation means making it easy for Google to understand what you do, where you do it, and why people should trust you, so it shows your business to the right searchers. For a small business, most of the value is in **local** searches like "accountant near me" or "roofer in Chester".

## The three things that matter most

### 1. A website Google can understand

Google reads the words on your pages. If your home page says "Welcome to our website" and nothing else, Google has nothing to work with. Each service you want to be found for needs its own page, with a clear title, a helpful description and answers to common questions. The site also needs to be fast, work well on phones and be secure.

### 2. Your Google Business Profile

For local searches, your Google Business Profile often matters more than your website. It decides whether you appear on the map. Choose the right primary category, fill in every section, add real photos, post updates and answer questions.

### 3. Reviews

Reviews influence both your ranking and whether people choose you. The best way to get them is simple: ask every happy customer, at the right moment, with a direct link. Reply to every review, including the negative ones, politely and promptly.

## What to ignore

- **Promises of guaranteed rankings.** Nobody controls Google.
- **Bulk backlink packages.** Low-quality links can harm your site.
- **Keyword stuffing.** Write for people, using the words they use.
- **Fake reviews.** They break UK consumer law and Google's rules.

## A simple plan for the next 30 days

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile.
2. Ask your last 20 happy customers for a review.
3. Check every main service has its own page with a clear title.
4. Set up Google Search Console and submit your sitemap.
5. Make sure your name, address and phone number match everywhere.
