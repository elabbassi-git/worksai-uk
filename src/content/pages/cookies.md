## Essential storage

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (key `wai-consent`) so we don't ask again. This is strictly necessary and needs no consent.

## Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics, we load Google Analytics 4 ({{GA4_ID}}), which sets cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*` to count visits and enquiries. These are not set unless you accept.

## Marketing

We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. {{AD_PIXEL_DETAILS}}

## Changing your choice

Use the **Cookie settings** link in the footer of any page to change your choice at any time.

## Third-party embeds

The booking calendar on our free audit page loads only after you click "Book". The provider ({{BOOKING_PROVIDER}}) may then set its own cookies.
