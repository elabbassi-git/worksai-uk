## When you don't need a custom CRM

Be honest with yourself first. You probably **don't** need a custom CRM if:

- you have fewer than about 50 active leads at a time and one person handles them (a well-organised spreadsheet or a free CRM tier is fine)
- your sales process looks like most others in your sector (an off-the-shelf CRM will fit)
- nobody on the team will own keeping it up to date (no CRM will fix that)

We will tell you this on the audit call, and we are happy to simply set up the free or cheapest option for you.

## When custom makes sense

- You sell in a way that big CRMs handle badly, such as multi-stage projects, repeat bookings or complex quotes
- Per-user licence fees for your whole team would cost more than a simple custom system
- You need the CRM to work closely with a [client portal](/business/client-portal/) or other systems we have built

Leads can flow in automatically from [AI agents](/services/ai-agents/) on WhatsApp, your website and the phone.
