## Custom portal or off-the-shelf?

| | Off-the-shelf portal | Custom portal |
|---|---|---|
| Set-up time | Days | Weeks |
| Fits your exact process | Partly | Fully |
| Monthly cost | Per-user licence | Hosting and support |
| Branding | Limited | Fully yours |
| Best for | Standard workflows | Unusual processes or many integrations |

We are happy to set up a good existing tool if it fits. A custom build makes sense when you would otherwise be paying for several tools and still working around them.

## Security and UK GDPR

A portal holds sensitive information, so security is part of the design, not an add-on: least-privilege access, encryption, two-step verification, audit logs, regular backups and a clear retention policy. We help you record it in your privacy notice and records of processing.
