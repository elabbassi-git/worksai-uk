## What it won't do

- It won't pretend to be a person.
- It won't give advice you haven't approved, such as medical, legal or financial guidance.
- It won't quote for complex work it can't price. Instead it books a call-back or a visit.
- It won't take card payments over the phone.

## Who it suits

AI reception suits businesses that miss calls because they are busy doing the work: trades, clinics, salons, garages, cleaners, removals, solicitors and estate agents. It pairs well with a [WhatsApp AI assistant](/services/ai-agents/whatsapp/) for customers who prefer to message, and with [online booking and reminders](/business/online-booking-system/) to cut no-shows. It is part of our wider range of [AI agents and virtual employees](/services/ai-agents/).
