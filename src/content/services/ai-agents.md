## What an AI agent is, in plain English

An AI agent is software that can read a message, understand what the person wants, look up the right information and take a simple action, such as booking a slot or saving a lead. It is not a person and it is not magic. It is very good at the repetitive first part of customer service, and it never gets tired of the same question.

We set each agent up with three things: **knowledge** (your services, prices, areas, policies and FAQs), **tools** (your calendar, CRM or booking system) and **rules** (what it may do alone and when it must hand over).

## Which assistant do you need?

Most businesses start with the channel where they lose the most enquiries:

- If customers mostly message you, start with a [WhatsApp AI assistant](/services/ai-agents/whatsapp/).
- If you miss phone calls, start with an [AI receptionist](/services/ai-agents/ai-receptionist/).
- If most leads come from your website, the same assistant can sit in a chat window on your site. That is included with our [website design and SEO](/business/website-design-seo/) package.

You can add channels later. The knowledge is shared, so a change to your prices updates every channel at once.

## When an AI agent is not the answer

If you get a handful of enquiries a week, or every job needs a long conversation before you can quote, a simpler fix may serve you better, such as an [online booking system](/business/online-booking-system/) or better follow-up on [late invoices](/business/automated-invoice-reminders/). We will tell you that on the audit call.
