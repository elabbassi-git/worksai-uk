## What makes a small business website work

A good small business website does three jobs: it **gets found** on Google, it **convinces** a visitor that you are the right choice, and it makes it **easy to get in touch**. Most sites manage one of the three. We build for all three.

- **Found:** each service gets its own page with a clear title, so Google can match it to searches like "boiler repair in Leeds" or "bookkeeper for sole traders".
- **Convincing:** plain-English descriptions, real photos, your prices or price ranges, and answers to common questions.
- **Easy to contact:** a phone number that can be tapped, a short form, WhatsApp, and an AI assistant for out-of-hours questions.

## Speed and mobile first

Most visitors will see your site on a phone. We design for small screens first and keep pages light so they load in a couple of seconds on a mobile connection. Speed matters to visitors and is one of the signals Google uses.

## SEO that is included, not an extra

SEO is not a mystery add-on. The on-page basics are part of the build: page titles and descriptions, headings, internal links, image descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and Search Console set-up.
