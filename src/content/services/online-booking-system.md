## How online booking cuts no-shows

Most no-shows are not rude customers. People forget, or something comes up and calling to cancel feels awkward. Good booking systems fix both problems: a reminder the day before, and a one-tap link to move the appointment. The slot is freed up in time for someone else to take it.

## Examples by type of business

These are **illustrative examples, not client results**:

- **Trades:** customers book a free survey or quote visit in a two-hour window. The booking asks for the postcode and a photo of the job, so you arrive prepared.
- **Salons and beauty:** clients choose the treatment and stylist. Longer treatments take a deposit, and reminders go out 48 and 2 hours before.
- **Consultants and coaches:** prospects book a 20-minute introduction call. The booking adds a video link and asks two questions so you know who you are speaking to.
- **Agencies:** clients book review meetings straight into the right team member's diary, with an agenda form attached.

Online booking works best with the rest of your customer journey. A [WhatsApp AI assistant](/services/ai-agents/whatsapp/) can answer questions and send the booking link.
