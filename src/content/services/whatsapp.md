## What a WhatsApp AI assistant actually does

Think of it as a well-briefed member of staff who only handles first replies. When a message arrives, the assistant reads it, works out what the customer wants and replies straight away. A typical conversation goes: greet the customer, answer the question, ask two or three qualifying questions, then offer a booking or a call-back.


## When it hands over to a person

A good assistant knows its limits. We set clear hand-over rules with you, for example:

- the customer asks to speak to a person, or sounds unhappy
- the enquiry is urgent, such as a leak, a lock-out or a same-day request
- the question is outside what the assistant has been told
- a price needs a site visit or a custom quote

When that happens, you get a notification with the full conversation and the customer is told someone will reply shortly. You can step into the chat from your phone or a shared inbox at any time.

## What you need to get started

- A business that gets enquiries by WhatsApp, or wants to
- Your current prices, services and the questions customers ask most
- A calendar or booking tool, if you want the assistant to book appointments
- About two hours of your time during the build, to check the answers

If you would rather the assistant lived on your website as well, it can. The same knowledge can power a chat window on your site, so customers get the same answer wherever they ask. Phone calls are covered by our [AI receptionist](/services/ai-agents/ai-receptionist/), and both sit under our wider [AI agents and virtual employees](/services/ai-agents/) service.

## Is it right for my business?

It works best when you get a steady flow of similar enquiries: trades, salons, clinics, tutors, cleaners, letting agents, venues and local services. If you get two messages a week, you probably don't need it yet. A better first step is often making sure people can find you at all, which is what our [local SEO and Google Maps](/services/local-seo/) service is for.
