## Why companies of 50 to 250 staff are different

Smaller businesses can pick one tool and go. Large enterprises have dedicated AI teams. Mid-sized companies sit in between: enough complexity to need a plan, not enough budget for a big consultancy programme. Our approach is to keep the strategy short and get to working systems quickly.

## What we look for in discovery

- Tasks done many times a week that follow a pattern, such as answering similar emails or processing similar documents
- Information that people search for but can't find
- Hand-offs between teams where work waits
- Reporting that is assembled by hand every month

## Governance from day one

Every roadmap includes a short AI use policy: which tools are approved, what data may be used with them, how outputs are checked, and who is accountable. It is written for staff to read, not for a filing cabinet.
