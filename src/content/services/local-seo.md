## What local SEO means for a small business

Local SEO is everything that helps you appear when people nearby search for what you sell. The most important part is your **Google Business Profile**: the box that shows your name, reviews, photos, opening hours and a button to call. It feeds both the map results and the panel on the right of Google search.

Your website matters too. Google cross-checks your profile against your site, so a fast site with clear service pages helps your map ranking. If yours is old or missing, see our [website design and SEO](/business/website-design-seo/) service.

## Your profile stays yours

We work on your profile as a **manager**, never as the owner. You keep full control and remove our access after the hand-over. If you don't have a profile yet, we help you claim or create one in your own name and walk you through Google's verification.

To qualify, you need an eligible location: either a place where you serve customers in person, or a business that travels to customers, such as a mobile hairdresser or electrician. We do not create listings at addresses you do not trade from.

## Pair it with a fast reply

More visibility means more enquiries. If you can't answer them all, a [WhatsApp AI assistant](/services/ai-agents/whatsapp/) or an [AI receptionist](/services/ai-agents/ai-receptionist/) makes sure each new lead gets a reply in seconds.
