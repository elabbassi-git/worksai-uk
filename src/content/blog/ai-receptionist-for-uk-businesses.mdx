## What an AI receptionist is

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant connected to your business phone number. When a call comes in, it answers, listens, understands what the caller needs and replies in a natural voice. It follows a script and knowledge base you approve, so it only says what you have told it to.

For a small UK business, the value is simple: **calls get answered** when you are on a job, with a client, driving or closed for the day.

## Hand-over rules: the most important part of the script

The difference between a helpful AI receptionist and an embarrassing one is almost always the hand-over rules. Write them down before you go live:

- **Emergencies:** for example a gas smell, flooding or someone in danger. The receptionist should give emergency guidance you approve, such as calling 999 or the relevant emergency line, and alert you immediately.
- **VIP or existing clients:** transfer straight to your mobile, or flag as priority.
- **Complaints:** take a full message and promise a call back from a person.
- **Anything outside the script:** take a message rather than guess.

## UK GDPR and call recording: what to check

An AI receptionist processes personal data: names, numbers, the reason for the call and usually a transcript. Under UK GDPR, you need to be transparent and proportionate. In practice:

<Checklist
  title="Data protection checklist for AI call answering"
  items={[
    'Tell callers at the start that the call is answered by AI and transcribed',
    'Mention call handling in your privacy notice',
    'Collect only what you need to deal with the call',
    'Limit who can read transcripts',
    'Set a retention period and delete old transcripts',
    'Use providers whose terms do not allow training on your data',
    'Know where call data is stored and processed',
    'Have a data processing agreement with your supplier',
  ]}
/>

This is general guidance, not legal advice. If you work in a regulated sector, check your specific obligations.

## How to prepare in a week

1. **List your top 20 call reasons** from the last month.
2. **Write short answers** to each, in the tone you use with customers.
3. **Define hand-over rules** for emergencies, VIPs and complaints.
4. **Connect your calendar** if you want bookings.
5. **Run practice calls** with friends before switching on forwarding.
