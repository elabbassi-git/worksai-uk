## What AI automation means for a small or medium business

Automation means software doing a task that a person used to do by hand. **AI automation** adds the ability to handle language and messy information: reading an email, understanding what a customer wants, pulling details out of a PDF or drafting a reply.

For a UK SME, that usually means fewer hours spent on admin, faster replies to customers and fewer things falling through the cracks.

## The test: what is worth automating?

Not every task should be automated. A good candidate scores well on four questions:

Tasks that need judgement, empathy or negotiation, such as handling a complaint or pricing a complex job, should stay with people. Automation can still help by gathering the information first.

## Seven automations UK SMEs typically start with

1. **Answering common enquiries** on WhatsApp or your website, with hand-over to a person. See our [WhatsApp AI assistant](/services/ai-agents/whatsapp/).
2. **Answering calls** when you can't, taking messages and bookings, with an [AI receptionist](/services/ai-agents/ai-receptionist/).
3. **Booking and reminders** that cut no-shows, through [online booking](/business/online-booking-system/).
4. **Chasing late invoices** automatically from Xero or QuickBooks, with [automated invoice reminders](/business/automated-invoice-reminders/).
5. **Logging leads** from forms, email and messages straight into your CRM.
6. **Reading documents** such as invoices, forms or ID and extracting the key fields for a person to check.
7. **Weekly reporting** that pulls numbers from several tools into one summary or dashboard.

## Data protection: automation and UK GDPR

Automations move personal data between systems, so the usual UK GDPR principles apply: only process what you need, know where it goes, keep it secure and don't keep it longer than necessary. When AI models are involved, check the provider's terms on data use and where processing happens.

## How to measure results

Before you automate, time the task for a week: how many times it happens and how long each takes. After automating, measure again. The saving is your proof, and it tells you which process to tackle next. Common measures include hours saved per week, response time to enquiries, days to get paid and no-show rate.
