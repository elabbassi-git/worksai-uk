## 4. Internal notes from the brief (not site copy)

- Pre-recorded courses sold to UK consumers are digital services; a non-UK seller must register for UK VAT (no threshold), unless a Merchant of Record platform sells, delivers and accounts for VAT. Live tutor-led teaching follows other rules: ask an accountant. Sales to businesses use the reverse charge.
- UK consumers generally have 14 days to cancel digital content and lose that right only if they clearly agree to immediate access: checkout needs a consent tick box.
- DMCC Act subscription rules (consumer subscriptions) are expected in force from January 2027. The site now sells no subscriptions; if recurring plans are ever reintroduced for consumers, they need pre-contract information, renewal reminders, cooling-off and easy online cancellation.
